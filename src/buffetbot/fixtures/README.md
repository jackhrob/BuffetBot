# Synthetic daily market fixtures

Every value here is fabricated. BBTEST is a fictitious ETF with a declared synthetic listing date. These are software fixtures, not validated historical prices or measured strategy returns. The actual exchange schedule is used solely to test session handling.

[accounting.json](accounting.json) contains explicit normalized source bars and action records. The prices reuse the independently qualified BB-002 input in `qualification/fixtures/daily.csv`. Bar interval boundaries, delayed availability and later ingestion are separate fields. Historical availability in this fixture is an authored assumption; ingestion in 2026 does not establish contemporaneous capture in 2024.

The declared July 1–9, 2024 window has six sessions: July 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 and 9. July 1 is warmup for the first July 2 execution. July 3 closes at 17:00 UTC (13:00 New York); July 4 is a holiday and July 6–7 are the weekend. Regular opens are 13:30 UTC, regular closes 20:00 UTC. Source bars become available 15 minutes after their respective closes. Nothing is inserted on closed dates.

[cases.json](cases.json) describes small explicit variations of those source records:

| Case | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| `accounting` | Publish six raw bars and two actions, with synthetic warning |
| `missing_bar` | Omit July 5; report the missing session and reject publication |
| `gap` | July 8 opens at 80 after a previous close of 58; preserve it and warn about the large gap |
| `zero_volume` | July 8 volume is zero; retain with warning under the declared allow policy |
| `incomplete_actions` | Declare action coverage unknown; reject publication even though two records exist |

## Independent accounting expectations

[expected-ledger.json](expected-ledger.json) records the expected **post-event** ledger at raw opening marks. It is authored input for subsequent accounting tests, never generated by the publisher. BB-004 stores the data and checks the research views; it does not implement the application's trading ledger. BB-002 qualified the same price/action scenario using the engine; BB-009 must integrate and verify it against these expectations.

Start with 10,000 USD, no shares and no receivable. Use 10 basis points adverse cost on each order, the qualified engine's cent-rounded quote prices, and 1 USD commission per order:

1. July 1: observe the warmup bar. Its close is 105, while the next opening price is 110. The overnight gap must not be replaced with the prior close when filling an order.
2. July 2: buy 10 shares at `110 × 1.001 = 110.11`. Cash becomes `10000 − 1101.10 − 1 = 8897.90`. Raw opening equity after costs is `8897.90 + 10 × 110 = 9997.90`.
3. July 3: the 2-for-1 split doubles holdings to 20 shares without changing cash. At raw open 56, equity is `8897.90 + 1120 = 10017.90`. Before this split becomes effective, the July 1 research close is 105; afterward it is 52.5. The raw close remains 105.
4. July 5: the regular 1 USD dividend establishes a 20 USD receivable for the shares held at the ex-date open. Cash stays 8897.90; raw opening equity is `8897.90 + 20 × 56 + 20 = 10037.90`. A buyer entering on the ex-date earns no entitlement.
5. July 8: sell all 20 at `60 × 0.999 = 59.94`. Cash becomes `8897.90 + 1198.80 − 1 = 10095.70`. The existing 20 USD receivable survives the sale; equity is 10115.70.
6. July 9: pay the 20 USD dividend exactly once. Final cash/equity is 10115.70, with zero shares and zero receivable.

The final change is `100 gross raw-price gain + 20 dividend − 2 commission − 2.30 adverse cost = 115.70 USD`. This arithmetic is an expectation for fabricated inputs, not a performance claim. Native engine opening-equity records taken **before** an order differ from the post-order marks above; future comparisons must use the same event timing.

Dividends are regular cash amounts per share held at the ex-date open, after any same-session split. Payment dates can fall beyond dataset coverage; subsequent accounting must retain the receivable and credit it on the first available session on/after the payment date. Reverse/fractional splits, cash-in-lieu, special dividends and other actions are unsupported.
